Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PromptifyAI's engines, quotas, and privacy architecture.

Is PromptifyAI free to use?
Yes! You get 35 free optimizations per day with the Free plan. No credit card required. Support via the Pro Supporter / Tip Tier for unlimited standard access.
Which AI platforms are supported?
We optimize for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, and DALL-E. Select your target platform inside the compiler before optimizing.
What's the difference between Standard and Advanced engines?
Standard uses Gemini Flash for low-latency structural optimizations. Advanced (Pro only) uses Gemini Pro for deep, multi-layered reasoning and nuanced refinements.
Can I sync my account across devices?
Yes. Sign in with Google to verify your supporter status and synchronize your account tier seamlessly across desktop and mobile browsers.
How fast is the optimization?
Most prompts compile within 2–5 seconds. Complex requests with 3-prompt matrix variations may take up to 10 seconds.
Is my data private?
Absolutely. We never sell your data. Only your Google profile (email and display name) is used for authentication and Pro status verification.
