Frequently Asked Questions

Is PromptifyAI free to use? ▼
Yes! You get 35 free optimizations per day with the Free plan. No credit card required. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited access.
Which AI platforms are supported? ▼
We optimize for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, and DALL-E. Select your target platform when optimizing.
What's the difference between Standard and Advanced engines? ▼
Standard uses Gemini Flash for fast optimizations. Advanced (Pro only) uses Gemini Pro for more detailed, nuanced refinements.
Can I save my prompts? ▼
Yes. Sign in with Google to sync your optimized prompts across devices. All prompts are stored securely in your account.
How fast is the optimization? ▼
Most prompts are optimized within 2-5 seconds. Complex optimizations with multiple variations may take up to 10 seconds.
Is my data private? ▼
Absolutely. We never sell your data. Only your Google profile (email, name) is stored for account management. Prompts are encrypted.
