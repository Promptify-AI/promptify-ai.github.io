Quick Support FAQ

What are the tool's daily usage limits?
Free users receive 35 standard prompt optimizations every 24 hours with zero credit card required. Pro Supporters enjoy unlimited daily executions on the Standard Engine (Gemini Flash) plus up to 100 high-compute executions daily on the Advanced Engine (Gemini Pro).
Is PromptifyAI free for students and independent developers?
Yes! PromptifyAI's core prompt compiler and our 35 daily optimizations are 100% free forever for students, researchers, and independent builders.
How can I report a bug or suggest a new target AI framework?
Select "Bug Report" or "Feature Request" in the form above, or email us directly at officialnotwinter@gmail.com with steps to reproduce the issue or details on the model you would like us to add.
How quickly is Pro Supporter status activated?
When you support PromptifyAI via Buy Me a Coffee using the same Google email address you use to sign in, your Pro tier perks (including 3-Prompt Matrix Mode) are linked to your account email. If you need manual verification, email us at officialnotwinter@gmail.com.
