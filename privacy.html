Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026

01 Introduction & Data Minimization Statement

At PromptifyAI (accessible from https://promptify-ai.github.io/), protecting your privacy is a foundational engineering principle. We adhere strictly to data minimization: we collect, process, and retain only the bare minimum information required to authenticate your session, enforce fair-use compute quotas, and compile your prompts. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers or third parties.

02 Information We Process

Depending on how you interact with PromptifyAI, we process the following limited categories of data:

  • Prompt Inputs: When you click "Optimize Prompt," your raw text input and selected target framework are transmitted securely via HTTPS to our serverless backend and processed transiently by AI APIs to return your optimized prompt. Your prompt inputs are not stored indefinitely by PromptifyAI in any persistent database.
  • Firebase Google Auth Profile Information: If you choose to sign in using Google Authentication, Firebase accesses basic profile metadata provided by your Google account—specifically your email address and display name—solely to authenticate your session and verify your Pro Supporter tier status.
  • Local Browser Usage Storage: PromptifyAI uses your browser's native LocalStorage and SessionStorage (such as promptify_date, promptify_usage_metric, and promptify_pro_model_metric) to track daily free and Pro execution counts locally, remember UI preferences, and maintain active authentication state across page reloads.

03 Google AdSense & Cookies Disclosure

"Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites."

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve advertisements to our users based on their visit to PromptifyAI and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings (https://www.google.com/settings/ads). Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance consumer choice portal at http://www.aboutads.info/.

04 Third-Party Infrastructure Disclosures

To operate reliably at scale, PromptifyAI relies on the following industry-standard third-party infrastructure providers, each governed by their respective security and privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure Google OAuth 2.0 user authentication and session management.
  • GitHub Pages & Vercel: Used for static frontend web hosting and serverless API routing.
  • Google Gemini API: Used as the underlying foundation model compute engine for processing and optimizing user prompts.
  • Google AdSense: Used to display non-intrusive advertisements that help fund free daily compute quotas for the community.

05 Data Security, Children's Privacy & Contact Information

Data Security: All data transmitted between your browser, our authentication providers, and our optimization endpoints is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

Children's Privacy: PromptifyAI is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately so we can purge such records.

Privacy Inquiries: If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to request deletion of your authentication record, or have data protection inquiries, contact us directly at: officialnotwinter@gmail.com.
