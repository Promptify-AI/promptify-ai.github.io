Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to PromptifyAI (https://promptify-ai.github.io/). Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Authentication (via Firebase), we collect your basic Google profile details including your name, email address, and profile picture strictly for account management, syncing saved prompts, and tracking daily usage limits.
  • Prompt Inputs: The raw ideas and text inputs you submit are processed via AI APIs (such as Google Gemini) to generate optimized prompts.
  • Log Data: Like most websites, standard non-identifying browser metadata (browser type, referring pages, timestamps) may be logged automatically by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages).

2. Cookies and Google AdSense

We use cookies and local storage to maintain your login session and preferences. Additionally, we may use third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on PromptifyAI based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads to our users.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. How We Use Your Data

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide and operate the PromptifyAI optimization engine.
  • Track your daily free tier usage or Pro account status.
  • Improve site performance and user experience.

4. Data Security

Account authentication and database management are handled securely via Google Firebase encryption standards. However, please avoid entering sensitive personal, financial, or confidential passwords into the prompt input box.
